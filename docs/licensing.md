# OpenStreetMap licensing and attribution

The generated basemap is derived from OpenStreetMap data provided by Geofabrik. OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Operational responsibilities include:

- Display `© OpenStreetMap contributors` anywhere the basemap is shown. The included MapLibre source supplies this attribution.
- Preserve required attribution when exporting screenshots, offline packages, or derivative products.
- Review the ODbL share-alike requirements before publicly distributing a modified database or substantial extract.
- Do not imply that OpenStreetMap or Geofabrik endorses DC Geomap.
- Observe Geofabrik download guidance and avoid needless downloads. The checksum gate and incremental-update preference are specifically intended to reduce traffic.
- Never bulk-download or use `tile.openstreetmap.org`; this system consumes the authorized regional PBF extract instead.

This document is operational guidance, not legal advice. Consult the current OpenStreetMap copyright/ODbL materials before external distribution.
